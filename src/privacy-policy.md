**Effective date:** June 11, 2026

Brooke Reports ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Mojo Blu LLC, a Utah limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit BrookeReports.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect very little, we don't sell anything we collect, and the only personal information we hold is what you hand us voluntarily — like your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you give us

**Newsletter signup.** If you subscribe to our email newsletter, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your name. That's it. We use Beehiiv to manage our email list, which means your email address is stored on their servers and subject to their privacy policy as well as ours.

**Direct contact.** If you email us, we'll have whatever you include in that email — your email address, your name if you sign it, and the contents of your message. We use this only to respond to you.

### Information collected automatically

**Server logs.** The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect and log technical information from visitors — such as IP addresses — for security and operational purposes. We do not have access to or control over this collection. You can review GitHub's practices in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**Cookies and tracking.** We do not currently run analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing pixels on the Site. We do not set cookies for tracking purposes. Our newsletter signup forms are provided by Beehiiv and may set their own functional cookies; refer to [Beehiiv's privacy policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy) for details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Send you the newsletter you subscribed to
- Respond to messages you send us
- Maintain and improve the Site
- Comply with legal obligations, if any arise

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your email address with third parties for their marketing purposes. Ever.

## 3. Email Communications

Every newsletter we send includes an unsubscribe link. Click it and you're out — no exit survey, no guilt trip, no "are you sure?" sequence. Unsubscribe requests are processed automatically through Beehiiv.

You can also email us directly at [support@mojoblu.com](mailto:support@mojoblu.com) to be removed from the list.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate the Site:

- **GitHub Pages** (hosting) — [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
- **Beehiiv** (newsletter delivery) — [Beehiiv Privacy Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy)

These services have their own privacy policies, and your use of features powered by them is subject to those policies.

The Site may contain links to external websites, including our related product, Second Look™ (secondlook.mojoblu.com), which has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites, even ones we like.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to the newsletter. If you unsubscribe, your address is removed from our active mailing list, though Beehiiv may retain suppression records to honor your opt-out (which is a good thing — it's how they make sure we can't accidentally email you again).

Emails you send us directly are retained as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it.

Because we collect so little, exercising these rights is simple: email us at [support@mojoblu.com](mailto:support@mojoblu.com) and tell us what you'd like done. We'll handle access, correction, and deletion requests for any information we hold about you, regardless of where you live.

**For EU/UK visitors (GDPR):** Our lawful basis for processing your email address is consent, which you provide at signup and can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.

**For California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at [support@mojoblu.com](mailto:support@mojoblu.com) and we will delete it.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, and we deliberately minimize what we collect in the first place — the most secure data is the data that doesn't exist. That said, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is material — meaning it actually affects how your information is handled — we'll make reasonable efforts to flag it, such as a note in the newsletter.
